Upcycling

Giving new life to found objects and forgotten materials.

Upcycling is the quiet art of seeing potential where others see clutter — turning worn, overlooked, or unexpected objects into something useful, beautiful, or surprising. It’s resourceful, creative, and rooted in the belief that a home grows richer when its pieces carry a story.
