Subfloor Reinforcement & Structural Adhesives

Older modular and dimensional-lumber floor systems built without cross-bracing can develop bounce, squeaks, and subtle vibration under heavy appliances and cabinets.

In our home: To stiffen our 26-year-old first-floor framing (23/32 OSB subfloor over 16-inch-on-center dimensional joists), silence a persistent squeak between the range and refrigerator, and stop the "curio cabinet effect" of glassware rattling when walking past, we have been installing solid wood cross-braces and sistered joists bonded exclusively with Loctite subfloor adhesive. We also keep other Loctite construction adhesives in the shop for projects where an uncompromising, gap-filling bond is non-negotiable.

Whole-Home Systems & Trades

Living on ten wooded acres means treating backup power, clean water, heating efficiency, and fuel storage as essential home infrastructure.

Backup Power & Electrical

Automatic standby power and dedicated circuits protect freezers, heating systems, and well pumps through rural winter storms.

In our home: During our recent renovation, we installed a whole-home Generac standby generator for uninterrupted power resilience. For electrical installations and dedicated appliance circuits, our trusted local electrician is Wilson Electric based in Jackson Center, PA.

High-Efficiency HVAC & Propane

Modern heat-pump systems paired with dependable fuel capacity cut utility costs while keeping the house comfortable through Western Pennsylvania winters.

In our home: We worked with Central Heating and Plumbing to install a high-efficiency Mitsubishi HVAC system that has significantly reduced our electric bills. For reliable fuel supply, we use Churchtown Propane, who installed our 1,000-gallon propane tank.

Whole-House Well Water Filtration

Addressing hard water minerals and hydrogen sulfide odor protects plumbing fixtures, appliances, and everyday water quality.

In our home: Our very first renovation project was solving severe hard water and sulfur ("rotten egg") odor with a whole-house multi-stage filtration system by WaterPro (paired with a Microline reverse osmosis system at the kitchen sink).
