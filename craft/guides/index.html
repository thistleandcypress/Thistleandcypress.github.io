Hanging Art & the Golden Ratio

Height, spacing, and proportion matter as much as the art itself.

How high to hang art — Apartment Therapy
How to create a gallery wall — West Elm
The golden ratio in interior design — The Spruce

As a rule of thumb, center art around 57–60 inches from the floor, and let the golden ratio (roughly 1:1.6) guide the balance between negative space and filled space.
