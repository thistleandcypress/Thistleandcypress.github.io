Painting

Walls, furniture, and the surfaces that shape a home.

Painting here is about the surfaces that carry color and character through a home — walls, trim, furniture, and the pieces that take on new life with a fresh coat. It’s practical, hands‑on work: refreshing what’s worn, choosing tones that set a mood, and transforming everyday objects with intention.

And sometimes, it’s about the art we bring into a room — the pieces that inspire us, influence a palette, or shift the feeling of a space. While this isn’t a place for fine‑art painting, it is a place for the creative decisions that shape how a home looks and feels.
