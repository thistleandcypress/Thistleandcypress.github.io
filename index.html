Welcome to Thistle & Cypress

We’re Rick and Jon—cooking, tending our ten acres in rural northwest Pennsylvania, and sharing a slower, more intentional way of living.

Here, we gather around honest, sensible food, explore the craft of learning by doing, move in step with the natural rhythms of our days, and follow the threads of good wine and travel back to the local cultures, stories, and friendships that inspire how we live.

This is your invitation to slow down, create with purpose, and root yourself in what matters.

Pull up a chair and find what speaks to you today.

Explore Our Core Practices

Over time, we’ve found our expression of the life we want to share taking shape across five main pillars that make up who we are: the kitchen, the garden, craft, rhythm, and wine & travel. Step inside each pillar and its sub-pillars to find practical experiments, stories, and lessons we've picked up along the way—and see what sparks something useful for your own table, projects, and daily life.
