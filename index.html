We are Rick and Jon, the founders of Thistle & Cypress — living between two coasts while building our future on ten wooded acres in Northwest Pennsylvania. When we step onto this land, we exhale.

Drawing on Scottish resilience (the thistle) and Italian abundance (the cypress), we are intentionally shaping the life we will fully step into at retirement — one rooted in food and wine, travel to Italy and Scotland, stewardship of the land, and alignment with the natural rhythm of the seasons.

Here you'll find recipes, garden plans, home projects, and reflections — organized through four living pillars that shape how we cook, grow, build, and live.

This is our table, our garden, our work — and we invite you to pull up a chair.
