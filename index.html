We are Rick and Jon, the founders of Thistle and Cypress--living between two coasts and building our future on 10 wooded acres in Northwest Pennsylvania. When we escape our busy West Coast careers to come here, we exhale.

Drawing on Scottish resilience (the thistle) and Italian abundance (the cypress), we’re slowly creating the life we’ll fully step into when we retire—one that includes travel to Italy and Scotland, immersion in food and wine, deep connection to this land, with appreciation for the rhythm of life.

If you are seeking a more unified life--where your home, garden, travel, and table feel like a single harmonious work of art -- you've found your people.

This is our table, our garden, our work—and we invite you to pull up a chair.

Explore the Four Pillars of Thistle & Cypress

Kitchen

Garden

Building a landscape from the ground up. From challenging soil to intentional plantings, we’re creating gardens, structure, and habitat across ten wooded acres.

Craft

The work of making a home: interior projects, seasonal creations, and the small handmade details that bring warmth and meaning to a space.

Rhythm

A life attuned to time and place — rituals, rest, and the steady pulse of seasonal living.
