Welcome to Thistle & Cypress

Here, we gather around honest food and wine, tend the land with care, shape the spaces we live in, and move in step with life’s natural rhythms — seasons, rituals, and the slow unfolding of experience.

It is an invitation to slow down, create with purpose, and root yourself in what matters.
