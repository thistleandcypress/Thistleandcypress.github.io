We are Rick and Jon, and when we step onto our ten wooded acres in Northwest Pennsylvania, we exhale. This is where we're building what comes next.

Drawing on Scottish resilience (the thistle) and Italian abundance (the cypress), Thistle & Cypress is our bridge to retirement—a life intentionally shaped around food and wine, stewardship of the land, travel to Italy and Scotland, and alignment with the rhythm of the seasons.

Whether you're years from your own transition or simply hungry for a more intentional life, we're documenting what we're learning—recipes, garden plans, home projects, and the honest work of building something lasting.

This is our table, our garden, our work. Pull up a chair.
