Rick's "No Nuts" Banana Bread

Four ripe bananas, two different treatments, and a gentle hand. The result is tender, fragrant, deeply banana-forward, and completely unconcerned with whether you remembered to buy walnuts.

Prep 15 min
Bake 50–75 min
Total 1 hr 30 min
Yield 1 loaf

Overview

This is the banana bread that came from years of experimentation: chunky mashed bananas for texture, whipped bananas for lift, and a gentle hand when the flour goes in.

The result is moist without being heavy, fragrant without being fussy, and deeply banana-forward. It is the sort of loaf that makes you briefly consider eating it with a spoon.

Ingredients

For the pan

  • Coconut oil spray, or any preferred non-stick option

Bananas + sugar

  • 4 ripe bananas
    • 2 mashed with a fork, chunky style
    • 2 for whipping into fluffy banana mousse
  • 1 cup sugar (or 1/3 cup for a lighter sweetness)

Wet ingredients

  • 3/4 cup melted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Dry ingredients

  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups double zero flour

Optional — still nut free, still fabulous

  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup dried fruit
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Method

1. Prep the Pan

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
  2. Grease a 9×5 loaf pan with coconut oil spray or your preferred non-stick option.

2. Mash the First Two Bananas

  1. Mash two bananas with a fork.
  2. Leave them chunky — texture is personality.

3. Whip the Other Two Bananas + Sugar

  1. In a mixer with a whisk attachment, whip the remaining two bananas with the sugar.
  2. Mix until it looks like banana mousse.

4. Add Wet Ingredients

  1. Add melted butter, eggs, and vanilla.
  2. Mix well and scrape the bowl thoroughly.

5. Add Dry Ingredients

  1. Add baking soda, salt, and flour.
  2. Mix gently — this is banana bread, not CrossFit.
  3. Fold in the mashed bananas and any optional add-ins.

6. Bake

  1. Pour the batter into your prepared pan.
  2. Tap the pan on the counter to release air bubbles.
  3. Bake for 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  4. Because oven temperatures can vary, start checking for doneness at 50 minutes and every five minutes thereafter. If it is browning too quickly, tent the top with foil until fully baked.
  5. If it cracks on top, congratulations — cracks are character.

7. Cool

  1. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes.
  2. Transfer to a rack to finish cooling.

8. Enjoy the Inevitable Compliments

Your kitchen now smells like you have your entire life together. Release any lingering stress, burn your fingers like the rest of us, and enjoy while pretending you'll "just have one slice."
