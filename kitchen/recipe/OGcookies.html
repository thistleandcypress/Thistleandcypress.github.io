Fall Spice Brown-Butter Gingerbread Oatmeal Cookies

Thick, chewy, brown-butter gingerbread oatmeal monster cookies featuring a 50/50 Caputo flour blend, dark molasses, and a robust spice profile.

Prep 25 min
Chill 1 hour
Bake 16–20 min
Oven 350°F
Makes 18–24 large

Overview

Forged through a rigorous AI quality control audit, these thick half-inch cookies balance deep molasses warmth and nutty brown butter with a tender crumb thanks to our custom 50/50 Caputo flour blend. Plumped raisins, old-fashioned oats, and a complex spice profile round out our ultimate autumn monster cookie.

A note from our kitchen: Every kitchen behaves differently—use your own judgment with ingredients, spice levels, and baking times.

Ingredients

Raisins

  • 1 ½ cups raisins
  • Water or orange juice, enough to cover for plumping

The 50/50 Caputo Flour & Dry Blend

  • 1 cup Caputo Traditional flour (Red bag)
  • 1 cup Caputo Baking flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 tsp cornstarch (our secret weapon for that soft, fudgy center)
  • 2 Tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 Tbsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • ½ tsp ground clove
  • 3 cups Bob's Red Mill old-fashioned oats

The Browned Butter & Sugars

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted Kerrygold butter, browned until nutty and amber, then cooled back down to a soft paste
  • 1 cup packed dark brown sugar
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup dark molasses
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract

Method

1. Plump the Raisins

  1. Place the raisins in a large measuring cup and cover them with water (or a splash of orange juice).
  2. Microwave for 1 minute to accelerate the plumping process.
  3. Drain thoroughly and pat the raisins completely dry with paper towels.

2. Brown the Butter

  1. Melt your Kerrygold butter in a saucepan over medium heat, swirling constantly until it foams, turns quiet, and forms amber-brown specks with a rich toffee aroma.
  2. Pour into a bowl and let cool until it reaches a soft, room-temperature paste.

3. Mix the Dry Ingredients

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the 50/50 Caputo flour blend, baking soda, salt, cornstarch, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and clove.
  2. Stir in the old-fashioned oats and set aside.

4. Build the Wet Base

  1. Using a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the cooled browned butter, dark brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth and creamy.
  2. Beat in the dark molasses, then add the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract, scraping down the bowl as needed.

5. Combine and Chill

  1. Slowly add the dry oat-and-flour mixture to the wet ingredients on low speed, mixing just until combined.
  2. Gently fold in your plumped, dried raisins.
  3. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate the dough for 1 hour.

6. Shape the Cookies

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and line heavy-duty baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Scoop massive, rugged, uneven mounds of dough using a large cookie scoop. Leave the tops craggy for texture.

7. Bake

  1. Bake for 16 to 20 minutes. About halfway through, gently rap the baking sheet once against the oven rack to create artisan ripples.
  2. Watch the edges; when set and matte while the center still yields slightly, pull them out.
  3. Let the cookies rest on the hot baking sheet for 5–7 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

Notes

The Brown Butter Factor: Browning the butter toasts the milk solids into a toffee-like backbone that elevates the gingerbread depth.

The 50/50 Flour Balance: Combining Caputo Traditional and Baking flours provides both structural backbone and tender interior chew.

The Pan-Bang Technique: Rapping the sheet halfway through baking creates those gorgeous surface wrinkles characteristic of artisan bakery cookies.
