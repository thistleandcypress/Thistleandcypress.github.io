Fall Spice Brown-Butter Gingerbread Oatmeal Cookies ("OG Cookies")

Our autumn mash-up forged in the AI Hell's Kitchen crucible: massive, thick, chewy monster cookies packed with browned butter, dark molasses, and old-fashioned oats.

Prep Time25 min
Chill Time1 hour
Bake Time16–20 min
Yield18–24 large

Ingredients

Raisin Prep

  • 1 ½ cups raisins
  • Water or orange juice (enough to cover for plumping)

The 50/50 Caputo Flour & Dry Blend

  • 1 cup Caputo Traditional flour (Red bag)
  • 1 cup Caputo Baking flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 tsp cornstarch (our secret weapon for that soft, fudgy center)
  • 2 Tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 Tbsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • ½ tsp ground clove
  • 3 cups Bob’s Red Mill old-fashioned oats

The Browned Butter & Sugars

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted Kerrygold butter, browned until nutty and amber, then cooled back down to a soft paste
  • 1 cup packed dark brown sugar
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup dark molasses
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract

Method

  1. Plump the Raisins: Place raisins in a large measuring cup, cover with water (or a splash of orange juice), and microwave for 1 minute. Drain thoroughly and pat completely dry with paper towels.
  2. Brown the Butter: Melt Kerrygold butter in a saucepan over medium heat, swirling constantly until it foams, turns quiet, and forms amber-brown specks with a rich toffee aroma. Pour into a bowl and let cool to a soft room-temperature paste.
  3. Mix the Dry Ingredients: Whisk together the 50/50 Caputo flour blend, baking soda, salt, cornstarch, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and clove. Stir in the old-fashioned oats and set aside.
  4. Build the Wet Base: Using a stand mixer with the paddle attachment, beat the cooled browned butter, dark brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth and creamy. Beat in the dark molasses, then add eggs one at a time, followed by vanilla extract.
  5. Combine and Chill: Slowly add the dry oat-and-flour mixture on low speed just until combined. Gently fold in the plumped, dried raisins. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour.
  6. Shape the Monster Cookies: Preheat oven to 350°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper. Scoop massive, rugged, uneven mounds of dough using a large cookie scoop, leaving tops craggy for texture.
  7. Bake: Bake for 16 to 20 minutes. Halfway through, gently rap the baking sheet once against the oven rack to create artisan ripples. Pull when edges are set and matte while the center still yields slightly.
  8. Cool and Serve: Let rest on the hot baking sheet for 5–7 minutes before transferring to a wire rack. Best enjoyed with a hot cup of coffee or tea.
A note from our kitchen: Forged through a rigorous AI quality control audit, these thick half-inch cookies balance deep molasses warmth with a tender crumb thanks to our 50/50 Caputo flour blend and browned butter. Use your own judgment and enjoy every bite!
