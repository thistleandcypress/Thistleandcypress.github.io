A Beautifully Cozy Organic Turkey Bolognese

A rich, savory turkey Bolognese with classic mirepoix and San Marzano tomatoes— hearty without being heavy, elegant enough for guests, and easy enough for a weeknight.

Prep 15 min
Cook 45–60 min
Total 1–1¼ hr
Yield 4–6 servings

Approximate Nutrition

300 calories
30g protein
20g carbs
12g fat
5g fiber
9g sugars

Approximate values per serving of sauce, based on 5 servings and the listed ingredients, including an estimated 2 tablespoons of olive oil. Actual values will vary with the turkey, tomatoes, oil, optional wine or broth, and portion size. Pasta and other serving accompaniments are not included.

Overview

A beautifully cozy bolognese made with organic ground turkey, a classic mirepoix, and the unmistakable depth of San Marzano tomatoes. Hearty without being heavy, elegant enough for guests, and exactly the kind of weeknight comfort that makes you feel like you're doing something good for yourself.

Ingredients

For the mirepoix

  • 1 cup finely diced carrots
  • 1 cup finely diced celery
  • 1 cup finely diced onion
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • Generous splash of good olive oil

For the sauce

  • 1–1½ pounds organic ground turkey
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 large can (28 oz) crushed San Marzano tomatoes
  • ½–1 cup broth or dry white wine (optional but lovely)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Small handful fresh basil or sprig of thyme
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Pinch of crushed red pepper flakes (optional)

To serve

  • Fresh pasta, gluten-free pasta, spaghetti squash, or creamy polenta
  • Drizzle of olive oil
  • Fresh basil for finishing
  • Parmesan (optional, if not dairy-free)

Method

🥕 1. Start with a Proper Mirepoix

  1. In a large Dutch oven, warm a generous splash of good olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Add carrots, celery, and onion.
  3. Sauté slowly until everything softens and turns glossy—about 8–10 minutes.
  4. This is the foundation of the sauce, so take your time. It should smell sweet and earthy.
  5. Add minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.

🦃 2. Brown the Organic Turkey

  1. Add organic ground turkey to the pot.
  2. Break it up gently with a wooden spoon and let it cook until no longer pink.
  3. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of crushed red pepper flakes if you like a little warmth.
  4. You want the turkey to pick up some color here—it adds so much flavor.

🍅 3. Build the Depth

  1. Stir in tomato paste.
  2. Let it cook for 2–3 minutes until it darkens slightly.
  3. This tiny step gives the sauce that deep, slow‑cooked taste.
  4. Pour in crushed San Marzano tomatoes.
  5. Stir everything together and bring it to a gentle simmer.

🍷 4. Let It Do Its Thing

  1. Add broth or wine (if using), bay leaf, and fresh herbs.
  2. Reduce the heat to low and let the sauce simmer uncovered for 30–45 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. It should thicken, deepen in color, and smell absolutely irresistible.
  4. Taste and adjust—more salt, more pepper, maybe a touch more basil.
  5. Trust your instincts.

🍝 5. Serve with Something Wonderful

  1. Ladle the bolognese over fresh pasta, gluten-free pasta, spaghetti squash, or even a bowl of creamy polenta.
  2. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and a scattering of fresh basil.
  3. If you're not dairy‑free, a little Parmesan is lovely—but truly, it's delicious either way.

6. And when someone inevitably says…

"Rick… this is the best bolognese I've ever had."

You can smile and say, "Oh, it's just something I whipped up."

Because honestly—how easy is that?
