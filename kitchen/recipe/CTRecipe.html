Rustic Harvest Chicken Braise

A cozy one-pot braise featuring crispy chicken thighs, sweet autumn root vegetables, and a velvety dill-tomato sauce cooked in our Le Creuset round braiser.

Rustic Harvest Chicken Braise

Cozy one-pot braised chicken thighs with sweet carrots, parsnips, gold potatoes, mushrooms, and olives in a velvety tomato-herb sauce.

Prep 25 min
Cook 35 min
Total 1 hour
Vessel Le Creuset Braiser
Serves 4
Calories 530
Protein 34g
Fat 32g
Carbs 28g
Fiber 5.5g
Sodium 680mg

Overview

Inspired by classic Mediterranean flavor profiles but rooted in autumn comfort, this one-pot braise transforms simple ingredients into something special. Sweet carrots, parsnips, and waxy potatoes simmer beneath golden, crispy chicken thighs in a rich tomato-dill sauce finished with tempered cream cheese and sour cream.

Kitchen Workflow Tip: Prepping and chopping your root vegetables ahead of time in a fridge bag makes assembly effortless. Line ingredients up on your counter in order of assembly before turning on the stove for a calm, stress-free cooking rhythm.
Salt Disclaimer: Salt preferences vary based on stock and canned ingredients. This recipe leans clean and balanced; taste your pan sauce before serving and adjust salt to your personal preference at the table.

Ingredients

Proteins & Fats

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Root Vegetables & Produce

  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced into ½-inch pieces
  • 2 tsp pre-diced garlic (or 3–4 fresh minced cloves)
  • 2 medium organic carrots, peeled and cut into ½-inch coins
  • 2 medium parsnips, peeled and cut into ½-inch coins
  • 2 medium yellow or waxy potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 can (6 oz) Native Forest Organic Mushroom Pieces & Stems, drained, rinsed, and dried

Accents & Liquids

  • 1 can (14.5 oz) organic diced tomatoes with juices
  • ⅓ cup whole or halved pitted olives
  • ½ cup low-sodium vegetable stock, warmed

Dairy & Finishes

  • 2 Tbsp cream cheese, softened to room temperature
  • 2 Tbsp Forager sour cream, room temperature

Herbs & Seasonings

  • 2 Tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped (divided: 1½ Tbsp for the braise, ½ Tbsp for garnish) + 1 sprig left whole
  • 1 Tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped (plus delicate whole fronds for visual garnish)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp freshly cracked black pepper

Method

1. Sear Chicken Skin-Side Down

  1. Pat the 4 chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels and season both sides with 1 tsp kosher salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in your Le Creuset braiser over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Place thighs skin-side down and sear undisturbed for 7–8 minutes until deep golden brown and the skin releases easily. Flip and sear for 2 minutes on the underside, then transfer to a clean plate.

2. Sauté the Root Vegetables

  1. In the rendered fat remaining in the braiser over medium heat, add the diced onion, carrots, parsnips, and cubed potatoes.
  2. Sauté for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the edges begin to soften and take on light golden color.

3. Add Aromatics, Tomatoes & Broth

  1. Stir in 2 tsp pre-diced garlic, drained mushroom pieces, the entire 14.5 oz can of diced tomatoes with juices, ⅓ cup olives, 1½ Tbsp chopped rosemary, and thyme leaves.
  2. Sauté for 1–2 minutes, scraping up any initial fond released by the tomato juices.
  3. Pour in ½ cup of warm vegetable stock and gently stir to combine into an even vegetable bed.

4. Simmer Covered (Low Heat)

  1. Nestle the chicken thighs (and accumulated plate juices) skin-side up on top of the vegetable bed, keeping the top skin resting above the liquid line.
  2. Cover tightly with the heavy lid, immediately drop heat to LOW to prevent scorching, and simmer gently for 20–22 minutes until potatoes and carrots are fork-tender and chicken reaches 165°F.

5. Temper Creamy Herb Sauce & Serve

  1. Remove the lid and turn off the burner. Lift chicken thighs briefly onto a warm plate.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk the softened cream cheese and room-temp Forager sour cream together. Ladle ~3 Tbsp of hot braiser liquid into the bowl, whisking vigorously to temper.
  3. Gently stir this mixture into the warm braiser around the vegetables until a rich, velvety sauce forms. Fold in 1 Tbsp chopped fresh dill.
  4. Nestle chicken thighs back on top, garnish with subtle fresh dill fronds and remaining chopped rosemary, and serve straight from the braiser!

Notes

The Le Creuset Advantage: The wide, shallow base of the round braiser allows maximum air circulation for crisp skin while keeping the root vegetables submerged in the braising liquid.

Off-Heat Tempering: Whisking hot broth into softened cream cheese and sour cream before adding it to the pot prevents the dairy from splitting in the acidic tomato sauce.

Leftovers: This dish keeps beautifully in the fridge—even the leftovers photographed wonderfully the next day!
