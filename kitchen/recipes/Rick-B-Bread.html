Prep: 15 minutes · Bake: 1 hour 15 minutes · Makes 1 loaf

Overview

A cozy, nut‑free banana bread with big flavor, easy technique, and that unmistakable “your life is together” aroma. Chunky mashed bananas, whipped banana mousse, and a gentle hand make this loaf tender, fragrant, and deeply comforting.

Ingredients

For the pan

  • Coconut oil spray (or whatever non‑stick magic you trust)

Bananas + sugar

  • 4 ripe bananas
    • 2 mashed with a fork (chunky style)
    • 2 for whipping into fluffy banana glory
  • 1 cup sugar (or 1/3 cup if you’re pretending to cut back)

Wet ingredients

  • 3/4 cup melted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Dry ingredients

  • 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups double zero flour

Optional (still nut free, still fabulous)

  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup dried fruit
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Method

1. Prep the Pan

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F.
  2. Grease a 9×5 loaf pan with coconut oil spray or your preferred non‑stick option.

2. Mash the First Two Bananas

  1. Mash two bananas with a fork.
  2. Leave them chunky — texture is personality.

3. Whip the Other Two Bananas + Sugar

  1. In a mixer with a whisk attachment, whip the remaining two bananas with the sugar.
  2. Mix until it looks like banana mousse and you briefly consider eating it with a spoon.

4. Add Wet Ingredients

  1. Add melted butter, eggs, and vanilla.
  2. Mix well and scrape the bowl like you mean it.

5. Add Dry Ingredients

  1. Add baking soda, salt, and flour.
  2. Mix gently — this is banana bread, not CrossFit.
  3. Fold in the mashed bananas and any optional add‑ins.

6. Bake

  1. Pour the batter into your prepared pan.
  2. Tap the pan on the counter to release air bubbles (and any lingering stress).
  3. Bake for 1 hour 15 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  4. If it cracks on top, congratulations — cracks are character.

7. Cool

  1. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes.
  2. Transfer to a rack to finish cooling… or slice early and burn your fingers like the rest of us.
