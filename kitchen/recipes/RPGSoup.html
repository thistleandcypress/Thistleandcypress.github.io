Roasted Red Pepper, Potato & Garbanzo Soup

Kitchen · Recipes · Serves 4

There’s a moment, right as the peppers blister and the potatoes turn golden, when someone inevitably wanders in and says, “Rick… what is that incredible smell.” This soup earns that reaction every time. Silky, vibrant, and deeply comforting, it layers roasted vegetables, warm spices, and fresh basil into something that tastes far more elaborate than it is. Every kitchen behaves differently—use your own judgment with ingredients, allergens, and doneness cues. Elegant enough for guests, cozy enough for a quiet night in—let’s make it together.

Prep: 15 minutes · Roast: 25–35 minutes · Simmer: 10 minutes · Serves 4

Overview

Every kitchen behaves differently—use your own judgment with ingredients, allergens, and doneness cues.

A silky, vibrant roasted soup built from blistered peppers, golden potatoes, warm spices, and a luxurious blend of basil and non‑dairy creamer. Deeply flavorful, beautifully simple, and endlessly comforting.

Ingredients

For roasting

  • 3 red bell peppers, quartered
  • 2 potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 can garbanzo beans, drained
  • 4–5 whole garlic cloves
  • Olive oil (generous drizzle)
  • Salt and pepper
  • A touch of cumin

For the broth

  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • Additional cumin
  • Red chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper

For blending

  • Fresh basil (a handful)
  • Non‑dairy creamer (to taste)
  • Warm stock (as needed for consistency)

Method

1. Roast Everything

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F.
  2. Arrange peppers, potatoes, garbanzo beans, and garlic on a large sheet pan.
  3. Drizzle generously with olive oil.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and a touch of cumin.
  5. Roast for 25–35 minutes, until caramelized and fragrant.

2. Build the Broth

  1. Warm the vegetable stock in a pot over medium heat.
  2. Add cumin, red chili flakes, salt, and pepper.
  3. Let it simmer gently while the vegetables finish roasting.

3. Blend Until Luxurious

  1. Transfer roasted vegetables and beans to a food processor.
  2. Add a couple cups of the warm stock, fresh basil, and a splash of non‑dairy creamer.
  3. Blend until smooth and silky.
  4. Add more stock or creamer until it reaches your perfect consistency.

4. Finish with Grace

  1. Pour the blended soup back into the pot.
  2. Simmer for 5–10 minutes to let the flavors settle.
  3. Taste and adjust seasoning—more basil, more heat, or a touch more creamer.
  4. Serve warm with a drizzle of olive oil, basil ribbons, and a pinch of chili flakes.

5. Listen to your friends and family say "Look at you!" when you serve this.
