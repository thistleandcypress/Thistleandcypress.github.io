Tortelli with Two Fillings & Brown Butter

Kitchen · Cooking · Serves 5

A New Year’s ritual: prepare and freeze handmade tortelli on New Year’s Eve, then cook them gently in brown butter and herbs on New Year’s Day.

Prep: New Year’s Eve · Cook: New Year’s Day

Overview

Handmade tortelli with two traditional fillings — potato and cheese, and sauerkraut with mushroom — finished in nutty brown butter with sage and thyme.

Every kitchen behaves differently—use your own judgment with ingredients, allergens, and doneness cues.

Dough

  • 400 g Italian 00 flour
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1–2 tsp olive oil (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Potato & Cheese Filling

  • 3–4 lbs potatoes (russet or Yukon Gold)
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2–4 tbsp butter
  • 6–8 oz sharp cheddar or farmer’s cheese
  • Salt and black pepper

Sauerkraut & Mushroom Filling

  • 16 oz sauerkraut, drained
  • 8 oz mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2–3 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 1–2 tsp caraway seeds (optional)
  • Splash white wine or broth (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper

Brown Butter Herb Sauce

  • 8–12 tbsp butter
  • 8–12 fresh sage leaves
  • Fresh thyme leaves (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano

Method

1. Make the Dough

  1. Form flour into a mound and create a well.
  2. Add eggs, salt, and olive oil into the center.
  3. Beat eggs with a fork, gradually incorporating flour.
  4. Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  5. Wrap tightly and rest 30–60 minutes.

2. Prepare Fillings

Potato & Cheese: Boil potatoes until tender. Sauté onion in butter. Mash potatoes and combine with onion and cheese. Season generously. Cool completely.

Sauerkraut & Mushroom: Sauté onion and mushrooms until browned. Add sauerkraut and optional seasonings. Cook 5–10 minutes. Cool completely.

3. Roll & Shape

  1. Divide dough into 4 pieces.
  2. Roll thin using pasta machine (setting 6–7).
  3. Cut into 2–2.5 inch squares.
  4. Add 1–1.5 tsp filling.
  5. Fold into triangle and seal edges.
  6. Wrap corners to form tortelli shape (optional).

4. Freeze

  1. Arrange in a single layer on lined trays.
  2. Freeze 45–60 minutes until firm.
  3. Transfer to freezer containers overnight.

5. Cook

  1. Bring well-salted water to a rolling boil.
  2. Cook tortelli directly from frozen.
  3. Once floating, cook 2–3 minutes more.
  4. Remove gently with slotted spoon.

6. Brown Butter & Finish

  1. Melt butter in wide skillet over medium heat.
  2. Cook until golden brown and nutty.
  3. Add sage and thyme; cook briefly.
  4. Toss tortelli gently in butter.
  5. Finish with grated Parmigiano and serve immediately.
