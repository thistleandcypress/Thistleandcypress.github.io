Christmas Eve Pasta Prep

Preparing fresh pasta by machine the day before Christmas Eve — a grounded, process-focused look at the work behind the meal.

The day before Christmas Eve carries its own kind of focus. This year, that focus centered on preparing fresh pasta by machine—simple ingredients coming together with a steady, deliberate rhythm.

Mixing the Dough

The dough followed a straightforward ratio of Italian 00 flour, water, and a small amount of olive oil. For anyone looking for a similar base formula, this extruded pasta dough guide from Lovely Bell Bakes offers a clear, well-tested reference. The mixture came together into a smooth, workable dough that held its shape without resisting the process.

Extruding the Pasta

Using the pasta machine changed the pace of the work. Instead of rolling sheets by hand, the dough moved through the mixer and die, emerging as long, even strands of fettuccine. There's a quiet satisfaction in watching the machine do exactly what it was built to do—steady pressure, clean cuts, and a growing pile of pasta ready for drying.

Drying and Finishing

Once extruded, the pasta was coated in semolina to prevent sticking, then transferred to racks to rest and firm up. Afterward, it was packed into a large zip-top container and refrigerated—ready to boil the following day. By the end, the kitchen felt organized and calm, everything in place for the next day's cooking. The work was simple, steady, and intentional, setting the tone for the meal to come.
