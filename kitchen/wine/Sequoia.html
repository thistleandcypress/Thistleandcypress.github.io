Set beneath its iconic stand of towering sequoias, Sequoia Grove Winery has become one of our most trusted Napa homes — a place where vineyard‑first winemaking meets warm, grounded hospitality. Located in the heart of Rutherford, the estate is known for crafting structured, expressive Cabernet Sauvignon that reflects the character of Napa Valley’s most celebrated vineyards.

What keeps us coming back, year after year, is the balance they strike between craft and comfort. The setting is peaceful — a grove of sequoias and cypress trees that feels worlds away from Highway 29 — and the team is consistently warm, knowledgeable, and genuinely invested in creating a memorable experience.

Their food and wine program is another standout. Sequoia Grove has long been known for its pairing experiences, and their new chef has elevated the offerings with thoughtful, seasonal dishes that highlight the structure and elegance of their wines. The Wine & Bites tasting remains one of our favorites: beautifully plated, educational, and always delicious.

As longtime club members, we’ve come to appreciate how seamlessly Sequoia Grove blends quality, consistency, and hospitality. Whether we’re picking up a release, settling in for a tasting, or joining a pairing experience, it always feels like returning to a place that knows us — and welcomes us back.

If you’re looking for a Napa winery that feels personal, grounded, and deeply connected to the land, Sequoia Grove is an easy recommendation. Their wine club offers access to limited‑production releases, member events, and priority reservations — but more importantly, it offers a sense of belonging to a place that values craft, community, and the quiet beauty of Napa Valley.
