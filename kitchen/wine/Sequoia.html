Sequoia Grove Winery

Rutherford Cabernet, Cabernet Franc, and a Napa Valley Tasting Experience We Return to Year After Year

Set beneath its iconic stand of towering sequoias, Sequoia Grove Winery has become one of our most trusted Napa homes — where vineyard-driven winemaking meets warm, grounded hospitality. Located in the heart of Rutherford, the estate is especially known for crafting structured, expressive Cabernet Sauvignon that reflects the character and depth of Napa Valley’s most celebrated vineyards.

What keeps us returning, year after year, is the balance they strike between precision and comfort. The setting is peaceful — a grove of sequoias and cypress trees that feels worlds away from Highway 29 — and the team is consistently warm, knowledgeable, and genuinely invested in creating a thoughtful experience. There is no pretense here, only confidence in craft.

Their Cabernets are unapologetically big — layered, structured, and built to age — yet they retain elegance and balance. We are particularly drawn to the depth of their Rutherford expressions, and their Cabernet Franc remains one of our perennial favorites: aromatic, textured, and beautifully composed.

The Food & Wine Experience

The culinary program is another standout. Sequoia Grove has long been recognized for pairing experiences that treat food and wine as equal partners at the table. With the addition of a new chef, the offerings feel even more intentional — seasonal dishes designed to highlight structure, acidity, and texture in the glass.

The Wine & Bites tasting remains one of our favorites: beautifully plated, educational, and a clear expression of how wine is meant to be experienced — in context, not isolation.

Planning Your Visit

Reservations for tastings can be made online through their tasting experiences page. If you are a member, the tasting fee is automatically reduced when booking.

Sequoia Grove is welcoming year-round. Summers in Napa Valley can be quite warm and are typically the busiest season for visitors. Spring and fall offer especially beautiful vineyard views and a slightly quieter pace.

Wine Club Membership

As longtime members of their wine club, we’ve come to appreciate how seamlessly Sequoia Grove blends quality, consistency, and hospitality. We are members of the Rare Reds club. Membership provides access to limited-production releases, member events, and priority reservations — but more importantly, it offers a sense of belonging to a place that values craft, community, and the quiet beauty of Napa Valley.

At Thistle & Cypress, we’re drawn to places where land, craft, and table converge. Sequoia Grove embodies that intersection. If you’re looking for a Napa winery that feels personal, grounded, and deeply connected to its terroir, it is an easy recommendation.

Disclosure: We are longtime wine club members of Sequoia Grove Winery. This post reflects our personal experience.
