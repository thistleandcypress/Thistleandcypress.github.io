The Sequoia Grove Series

Part 1: The Intersection of Land, Craft, and the Rutherford Bench

Our journey with Sequoia Grove Winery is one of the more pivotal moments in our wine journey. We are naturally drawn to what we call “big reds”—wines with substantial tannin, beautiful balance, and deep flavor profiles. We’ve been wine club members here for several years, a journey that began after being introduced to the winery by our good friends Julie and Christi.

Terroir and the Single Vineyard Series

Their reds, primarily the Single Vineyard Series, are our perennial favorites. Why? Because you can truly feel the differences between grapes grown in different parts of Napa Valley. The winery itself sits on the Rutherford Bench—a specific type of gravelly soil that gives their estate wines their unique, dusty tannins.

They also source from celebrated vineyards like Stagecoach, Lamoreaux, Christian, and Tonella—each offering unique characteristics that we love. Jon is always drawn to a strong, tannin-forward Cabernet Franc (the traditional backbone for red blends), and Sequoia Grove's expression of this varietal has consistently hit the mark for us year after year.

Our Cellar & The Rare Reds Community

We have been members of the Rare Reds Wine Club since the beginning. We always look forward to the new finds offered each time we pick up. Our personal cellar is built around a belief in this estate’s aging potential, and we are currently tracking several key labels in our library:

  • The Cambium: Their flagship blend and the pinnacle of Sequoia Grove winemaking—a powerful expression of the best fruit from the vintage.
  • Tonella Vineyard Cabernet Franc: A masterclass in aromatic complexity. We enjoy comparing current releases against our 2012 library bottles.
  • Morisoli Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon: The quintessence of "Rutherford Dust"—dark fruit and impeccable structure.
  • Stagecoach Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon: A mountain-grown Cabernet from Atlas Peak that offers a rugged contrast to the valley floor.
  • Lamoreaux Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon: An elegant Oak Knoll District expression that rounds out our collection.

The Elevation of the Culinary Program

Perhaps one of the most significant improvements we’ve noticed since becoming members is the elevation of their food program. Their current chef, Spencer Conaty, has elevated the Wine Tasting experience—whether it’s sophisticated snacks during a flight or their full food and wine pairings. We highly recommend them all!

Take some time to learn more about their story. As it says right on their website, they strive to honor the past while staying committed to the future. We can’t wait to bring you more from our experiences here at Sequoia Grove Winery in the chapters to come.

Disclosure: We are longtime wine club members of Sequoia Grove Winery. This post reflects our genuine appreciation for their craft and hospitality.
