The Sequoia Grove Series

Chapter 1: The Intersection of Land, Craft, and the Rutherford Bench

There is a specific quiet that exists under the canopy of a sequoia grove. In the heart of Napa Valley’s Rutherford Bench, Sequoia Grove Winery has managed to bottle that stillness. For us, this isn't just a stop on a map; it’s a decade-long study in terroir, hospitality, and the evolution of the California Cabernet.

The Portfolio: A Study in Terroir

To understand Sequoia Grove, you must understand the Rutherford Bench. The well-drained gravelly soils here produce what winemakers call "Rutherford Dust"—a fine-grained tannin structure that gives the wines a cocoa-powder finish. While their Napa Valley Cabernet is a benchmark, the true depth of the estate is found in their high-tier offerings.

From the Cellar: The Collection

Our personal cellar is built around a belief in this estate’s aging potential. We are currently tracking the evolution of several key labels, laying the foundation for a series of upcoming vertical tastings and pairing reviews:

  • The Cambium: We have several vintages of this flagship blend in our library. It represents the absolute pinnacle of Sequoia Grove winemaking—a powerful, seamless expression of the very best fruit from the vintage.
  • Tonella Vineyard Cabernet Franc: Comparing the vibrant 2019 against our 2012 library bottle continues to reveal the incredible aging capacity of this varietal.
  • 2019 Morisoli Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon: The quintessence of "Rutherford Dust"—dark fruit, historic site, and impeccable structure.
  • 2021 Stagecoach Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon: A mountain-grown Cabernet from Atlas Peak that offers a rugged, intense contrast to the valley floor.
  • 2021 Lamoreaux Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon: An elegant Oak Knoll District expression that rounds out our collection with its bright, lifted fruit.

The Culinary Destination

Sequoia Grove has transitioned from a traditional tasting room to a premier culinary destination. The introduction of their estate chef has transformed the experience into a technical demonstration of how food and wine interact.

The Reserve Your Wine + Bites Tasting is the perfect entry point for the "food as a second glass" philosophy. In future chapters of this series, we will be doing a deep-dive feature on the Chef's seasonal pairings and how we recreate the Sequoia "Estate Table" experience in our own kitchen.

Rare Reds Membership

As members of the Rare Reds Club, we’ve found a community that values craft over pretense. If you’re looking for a Napa home that feels personal, grounded, and deeply connected to its terroir, Sequoia Grove is where you begin.

Disclosure: We are longtime wine club members of Sequoia Grove Winery. This post reflects our genuine appreciation for their craft.
