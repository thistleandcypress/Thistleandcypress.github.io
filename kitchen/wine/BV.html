The 2022 BV Cabernet is our go-to for a daily drinking wine. It is one of those wines that reminds you why Napa became famous in the first place. It opens with that unmistakable dark‑fruit core—blackberry, black cherry, maybe even a little plum—but nothing feels heavy or overdone. It's confident without trying too hard. It also has a more budget-friendly price point, compared to siliar wines.

What stands out most is the balance. The tannins are present, but they're not the kind that take over your whole mouth. They're smooth, almost polished, giving the wine enough structure to feel serious while still being easy to drink on a weeknight. There's a gentle warmth from the oak—vanilla, a little cocoa, something toasty—but it never crosses into that overly sweet territory some Cabernets fall into.

I'm sure a professional taster would describe layers of things like cedar or graphite or forest floor. What I notice is simpler: it tastes clean, well‑made, and pleasantly complex. Each sip feels slightly different depending on what you're eating. With something salty, the fruit pops more. With something rich, the wine feels deeper and more grounded. It's versatile in a way that makes it feel like a reliable companion rather than a wine you have to plan a meal around.

Overall, the 2022 BV Cabernet is exactly what you want at this price point: classic Napa character, smooth edges, and enough personality to keep you interested without demanding your full attention. It's the kind of bottle you finish without realizing it—and that's usually the best sign.
