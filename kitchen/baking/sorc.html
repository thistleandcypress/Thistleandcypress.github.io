Fall Spice Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Warm, chewy, and heavily spiced cookies—celebrating the shift in seasons with a custom mash-up born right from our kitchen table.

There is a distinct rhythm to how we welcome autumn at Thistle & Cypress—it always begins when the air turns crisp and the kitchen starts filling with the scent of warming spices.

It all started when Rick put in a special request. As a longtime devotee of both hearty oatmeal raisin cookies and deep, spiced bakes, he wondered if we could combine the two into the ultimate autumn cookie mash-up. Taking our cue from a beloved Brown Eyed Baker classic, we set out to build a recipe that leans entirely into that cozy craving. While we didn't happen to have molasses or cloves on hand for a traditional gingerbread direction, what emerged from our mixing bowls was something even better: a pumpkin-pie-meets-oatmeal masterpiece heavily loaded with spice, baked large, and kept wonderfully soft and chewy in the center.

🌾 The Importance of Quality Ingredients

When you're working with classic flavors, the components you choose make all the difference in the final texture and depth. We kept the core measurements of our trusted foundation, but carefully upgraded each element:

  • The Flour: We swapped in Caputo 00 flour (the red bag). Its fine grind creates an exceptionally tender, delicate crumb that sets these apart from your average cookies.
  • The Oats: Bob's Red Mill old-fashioned oats provide that essential, rustic backbone and hearty texture.
  • The Butter: Rich, premium Kerrygold butter, whipped up effortlessly in the KitchenAid stand mixer to create a velvety, rich base.
  • The Raisins: Nobody likes a dry raisin. We gave ours a quick 1-minute water bath in the microwave to plump them back to juicy perfection, then patted them completely dry before folding them into the dough.
  • The Warming Spices: To capture that true autumn spirit, we went heavy-handed with the spice blend—about 5 tablespoons of cinnamon, alongside a full tablespoon each of ginger and nutmeg. The robust warmth pairs masterfully against the sweet raisins and wholesome oats.

👩‍🍳 How We Made Them

  1. Plump the raisins: Toss your raisins into a large measuring cup, cover with water, and microwave for 1 minute to accelerate the plumping process. Pat them dry thoroughly before folding them in later.
  2. Mix the base: Using your KitchenAid stand mixer, cream the Kerrygold butter and sugars until light, pale, and fluffy.
  3. Incorporate wet ingredients: Add your eggs and pure vanilla extract, mixing thoroughly.
  4. Blend the dry ingredients: Whisk together the Caputo 00 flour, baking soda, salt, and your heavy-handed ratios of cinnamon, ginger, and nutmeg.
  5. Combine and chill: Gently fold in the Bob's Red Mill oats and your plumped, dried raisins. Let the dough rest in the refrigerator to deepen the flavors and hold its shape.
  6. Bake large and slow: Scoop generous portions of dough. Because of their size, they take about 28 minutes to bake through, keeping the edges lightly golden while the centers stay delightfully soft and chewy.

🍽️ Celebrating Fall Together

Everyone in our circle who has tried this batch has fallen instantly in love with the deep autumn spice notes. Whether you enjoy them warm out of the oven with a hot cup of coffee or packed away for a crisp afternoon walk, they capture the very heart of seasonal baking here at Thistle & Cypress.
