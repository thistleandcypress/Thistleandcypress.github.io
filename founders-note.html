Founders’ Note

A note from Jon and Rick — the life behind Thistle & Cypress.

Home About Kitchen Garden Craft Rhythm

This is the heart behind Thistle & Cypress — why we built it and what we hope it becomes.

Thistle & Cypress is the way we live: cooking and baking in a kitchen shaped by memory and travel, exploring wine and the stories it carries, and tending gardens from the ground up as we rebuild a blank landscape in Pennsylvania. We’re learning the land one tree, shrub, perennial, and herb at a time — and sharing what we discover along the way.

We’re also makers at heart. Florals, wreaths, paint, upcycled furniture, and even modern creative tools like AI all find their place here. Creativity, for us, is both craft and curiosity.

And woven through it all is rhythm — the daily rituals, mindful moments, and seasonal shifts that give shape to a life.

This space gathers those threads: the kitchen, the garden, the craft, and the rhythm of the year. Our hope is that Thistle & Cypress offers ideas, guidance, and a sense of rootedness as you shape a life that feels both grounded and expansive.

—Jonathan & Rick
