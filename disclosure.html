We believe trust is more valuable than any commission. This page explains how recommendations are made on Thistle & Cypress, how affiliate relationships work, and the principles that guide our editorial decisions.

Our Philosophy

Thistle & Cypress grew from real life: meals shared around the table, projects completed in the workshop, seasons spent tending the garden, travels undertaken with curiosity, and the ongoing work of creating a thoughtful home.

The products, tools, ingredients, books, equipment, and resources mentioned throughout this site appear because they are part of that life. They are not selected simply because a commission may be available.

Affiliate Relationships

Some pages on this website may include affiliate links. This means that if you choose to make a purchase through one of these links, Thistle & Cypress may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

These commissions help support the ongoing operation of the site, including research, writing, photography, hosting, maintenance, and the creation of new content.

How Recommendations Are Made

Whenever possible, we recommend products, tools, ingredients, and resources that we use ourselves, have tested directly, or believe provide genuine value based on our experience and research.

Not every product will be the right choice for every reader. Needs, budgets, preferences, and circumstances vary widely. Our goal is not to tell you what to buy, but to share what has worked for us and why.

Editorial Independence

Compensation does not determine what appears on this site. Affiliate relationships do not control our opinions, recommendations, conclusions, or experiences.

We do not guarantee favorable coverage in exchange for compensation, and we do not present sponsored recommendations as independent opinions.

House Standards

Throughout Thistle & Cypress you may encounter references to products and brands that have earned a permanent place in our kitchen, garden, workshop, home, or travels. These "house standards" represent items that have demonstrated usefulness, durability, reliability, or lasting value in our own experience.

Their inclusion reflects trust earned over time, not marketing obligations.
