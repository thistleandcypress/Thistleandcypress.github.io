Resources

Tools, studies, frameworks, and supports for a grounded, intentional life.

Rhythm is a practice of living with intention — moving in alignment with your values, honoring the natural cycles that shape your days, and finding steadiness through moderation, balance, and mindful awareness. This page gathers the studies, frameworks, and external references that support the ideas explored throughout the Rhythm pillar. It’s a single, evolving library you can return to whenever you want to go deeper into the patterns, symbols, seasons, and grounding practices that help shape an authentic, well‑paced life.

The resources listed here include third‑party websites, books, studies, and tools. They are provided for informational purposes only and may change, move, or become unavailable over time. Thistle & Cypress does not control or endorse external content, and is not responsible for the accuracy, availability, or practices of any linked sites. Always use your own judgment when exploring outside sources, and consult qualified professionals when guidance is needed.
