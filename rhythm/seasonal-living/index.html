Seasonal Living

Letting the natural cycles of the year guide our pace, presence, and perspective.

Seasonal living is the practice of moving with the year instead of against it — noticing how light shifts, how weather shapes our days, and how each season invites a different kind of energy. These reflections explore the rhythms of winter rest, spring renewal, summer abundance, and autumn grounding, offering gentle ways to align life with the world outside our door.

The sun's seasonal rhythm

The sun has been keeping this rhythm since long before any of us were here to notice it.
