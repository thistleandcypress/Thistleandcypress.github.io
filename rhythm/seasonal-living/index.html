Living with the turning year.

The year moves in a steady, beautiful circle—from the quiet dormancy of winter to the bursting energy of spring, the long warmth of summer, and the rich gathering light of autumn. Seasonal living is the art of tuning into these larger turns, allowing our homes, our habits, and our inner pace to shift in harmony with the natural world.

The Sun’s Seasonal Rhythm

The sun has been keeping its rhythm since long before any of us were here to notice it. Explore the analemma, the changing path of sunlight through the year, and the ancient seasonal patterns that still shape the light in our homes today.
