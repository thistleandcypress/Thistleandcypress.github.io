Who We Are

We split our time between San Francisco, where our careers, friends, family, and favorite farmers markets and restaurants are, and ten acres of wooded land in northwest Pennsylvania. The land has been in Jon’s family for generations, and when he inherited it, we both knew immediately: this was where we’d build our future. Not someday — now.

For the moment, we move back and forth. Weeks in San Francisco, then back to Pennsylvania whenever we can steal the time. It’s not perfect, but it’s the bridge between the life we have and the life we’re creating. Every trip, we exhale a little deeper. Every project brings us a little closer.

We met in Washington, D.C. more than thirty years ago, and over three decades we’ve supported each other through career pivots, cross‑country moves, and the slow realization that the life we wanted wasn’t going to happen by accident. This land — this project — is us finally taking that truth seriously.

The name Thistle & Cypress reflects where we come from. Jon’s family is Scottish — resilient, resourceful, a little rough around the edges. Rick’s is Italian — warm, generous, deeply rooted. The thistle and the cypress. Together, they represent who we are and what we’re building: a life that’s both rugged and refined, practical and beautiful, grounded and expansive.

We’re practitioners, not experts. Rick is the intuitive cook who doesn’t measure; Jon is the recipe collector who can’t resist sending one more idea at midnight. Rick builds; Jon plans. We both plant, preserve, and make mistakes in the garden. We travel to Italy and Scotland not just because they’re in our heritage, but because they remind us what’s possible when culture, craft, and place come together.

We believe a good life is built slowly, through four pillars: Kitchen (the food we make and share and the wine we drink), Garden (the land we tend), Craft (the things we build with our hands), and Rhythm (the rituals and seasons that shape our days). These aren’t aspirational ideas — they’re what we’re actually living, one season at a time.

If you’re building something similar — a slower, more intentional life that reflects your values — we’re here as guides and companions. Not because we have all the answers, but because we’re a few steps ahead on the path, and we remember what it felt like to wonder if this kind of life was even possible.

It is. And we’d love for you to join us.
