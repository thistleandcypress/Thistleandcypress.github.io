Rick & Jon — Welcome to Thistle & Cypress

This brand honors our roots. The thistle represents Jon’s Scottish heritage (resilient, resourceful, enduring). The cypress represents Rick’s Italian lineage (warm, generous, deeply tied to place). Together, they shape a life that’s rugged yet refined, practical yet beautiful.

We’re practitioners, not experts. What we share — always — is a love of good food, the joy of seeing the land thrive, and a belief that travel is essential inspiration for how we live at home. We plant, build, preserve, make garden mistakes, and learn from them.

At the heart of our days are five interconnected pillars. They don't exist in isolation; they flow constantly into one another — where a harvest from the soil guides an evening meal, and a handmade object establishes the quiet rhythm for a season. Together, they form the ecosystem of our home:

  • Kitchen — where seasonal ingredients, intuitive cooking, and thoughtful wine invite us to slow down and share.
  • Garden — the patient tending of soil and growth that connects us directly to the timeline of the earth.
  • Craft — the deliberate work of our hands, creating meaningful objects and physical spaces that hold a story.
  • Rhythm — the quiet rituals, seasonal shifts, and mindful pauses that bind these practices together into a purposeful life.
  • Wine & Travel — the exploration of terroir, culture, and craft that broadens our palate and inspires our daily life.

If you’re quietly shaping a slower, values-driven life—experimenting, savoring, learning—we’re on a parallel path. Not as authorities, but as companions sharing what we’ve found so far.

We’re glad you’re here. Pull up a chair.
