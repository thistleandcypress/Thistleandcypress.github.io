Annuals & Biennials

Quick color, seasonal rhythm, and short-lived brilliance

The Annuals & Biennials section gathers the plants that live fast and bloom hard — those that complete their life cycle in one or two seasons. These are the showy companions to longer-lived perennials, offering bursts of color, texture, and movement. Whether reseeded each year or planted with intention, they bring immediacy and rhythm to the garden’s evolving story.

Seeds are planted and awaiting the spring thaw. This corner of the garden is taking root.
