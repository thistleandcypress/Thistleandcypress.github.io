The garden begins with poor soil and possibility. We’re learning the land as it is — its challenges, its rhythms, its quiet potential — and shaping it into something new. One design choice, one planting, one season at a time, we’re building a landscape that will grow with us: orchard, garden, paths, and the habitats that make this place feel alive.
